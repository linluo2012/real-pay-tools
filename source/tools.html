How these tools work

  • Nothing leaves your device. All calculation happens in your browser with JavaScript. There is no server request, no account, and no record of what you entered.
  • Every result shows its working. Each tool displays the brackets, rates and assumptions used, so you can check the arithmetic yourself.
  • Figures are dated. Tax data is labelled with the tax year it applies to and the date it was last reviewed.
  • Estimates, not filings. Payroll depends on your W-4, benefits elections and local rules. Use these tools to understand an offer, then verify anything binding with a professional.
