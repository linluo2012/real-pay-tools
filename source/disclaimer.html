Tax figures

Federal calculations use tax year 2026 brackets and the standard deduction from IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-32, and FICA rates from IRS Publication 15 and the Social Security Administration. Actual payroll withholding depends on your Form W-4 entries, pre-tax and post-tax deductions, local taxes, and changes during the year. State figures are representative effective rates and are not exact statutory schedules.

Employment decisions

Severance, offer comparison and pay figures are general estimates. Severance is not legally required in most US circumstances and policy varies widely between employers. Equity valuations are speculative and depend on vesting, liquidity and company performance. Nothing here should be relied on to sign or reject an offer, a separation agreement, or a contract.
